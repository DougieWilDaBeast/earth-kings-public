# What was made with AI

Earth Kings' character, creature, building and terrain art was made with PixelLab, a generative pixel-art tool, on the developers' own account, from prompts the developers wrote, and was then cleaned up and arranged by the team. The frames of the menus, text boxes and buttons were made with Google's Nano Banana image model on the developers' own account, from the developers' prompts and screenshots of the game, then cut and cleaned up by the team. Two fight effects (a lightning bolt and a fire pillar) come from another developer's MIT-licensed set, which they made with ChatGPT's image model. Some scenery, effects and all of the game's own sound effects and placeholder music were drawn or synthesised by code written with the help of an AI assistant (Claude); no image or audio model was used for those. Most of the in-game text (dialogue, story chapters, item and place descriptions, tips) was drafted by an AI assistant (Claude) from the developers' own recorded notes and design decisions, and every story choice in it was made by the developers. The game's main music themes were composed by a developer.

Nothing in the game uses AI while it runs: every picture, sound and word is in the build as made.
